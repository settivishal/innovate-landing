How it works

Talk while you walk.

No forms, no typing, no photos to sort at the end of the day. Kira does the paperwork while you do the inspection.

Beyond inspection

From finding problems
to fixing them.

Kira doesn't stop at the report. The same assistant guides installs, teaches new managers and remembers every walk.

Laser guidance

Projected installation instructions

The laser projector puts anchor points, cut lines and install sequences onto the surface at full scale. Crews follow the light.

Learning

Learn on the walk

Point at an air-handling unit or an electrical box. Kira explains what it is, how it fits the system, and what your SOPs say to check and photograph.

Project memory
