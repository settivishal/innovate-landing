How it works

Talk while you walk. Kira handles the paperwork.

Walk

Start a walkthrough. Kira comes online and watches the live camera.

Talk

Say "Hey Kira" and describe what you see. No typing, no forms.

Confirm

Kira drafts the note, snaps the photo and asks for a priority. You decide.

Report

End the walk and get a summary, action items and a daily report.

Platform

Everything a site walk needs, in one assistant

Voice-first notes

Speak naturally. Kira turns it into a structured note with category, priority and an action item.

Live hazard detection

Missing hard hats, unsafe ladders, flammable storage and blocked exits, boxed on screen as you walk.

Photo evidence

Every note carries the frame it was recorded on, time-stamped and tied to the walkthrough.

Daily reports and briefs

Summaries, issue logs and action lists for managers and supervisors, ready as PDF or CSV.

Follow-ups with approval

Kira drafts the email to the right trade. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

Schedules and drawings

Kira reads your schedule and drawings, answers questions on site and drafts schedule updates from past projects.

A walkthrough with Kira

An assistant, not an alarm

Kira points things out. You make the call. Hazards she spots stay suggestions until an inspector confirms them, so every record is one you stand behind.

  • Human-confirmed records. AI flags never become official notes on their own.
  • Full audit trail. Every note, photo, flag and reply is logged in order.
  • Stays on task. Kira only helps with the inspection in front of you.

Beyond inspection

From finding problems to fixing them

Laser guidance

Projected installation instructions

Pair Kira with the laser projector to put anchor points, cut lines and install sequences directly onto the surface. Crews follow the light, and Kira checks the result.

Training

On-site teaching module

Guided lessons for new workers, built on real site footage. Kira walks trainees through tasks step by step and checks their work as they go.
